Overview

Monitoring we built to protect our own vehicles

CAMVER has run its own vehicles for six years and now operates 145 of them. Fleet AI OS is built on our own AI engine, trained on the driving data gathered over that time.

It collects the data each vehicle sends, reads risks ahead of time, and controls the vehicle remotely when needed. However many vehicles you have, you see them all on one monitoring dashboard.

What the monitoring dashboard does

    See vehicle location and driving status in real time, with an around view monitor (AVM) to reduce blind spots.

  • Vehicle management

    Driving records and patterns build up as data for each vehicle, helping cut operating costs and manage drivers.

  • Environment sensors

    Six readings, such as cabin temperature and carbon monoxide, help prevent accidents before they happen.

  • Remote control

    Check vehicle location and control the vehicle remotely from an app or PC.

How It Works

From vehicle data to an early alert

  1. 01

    Collect

    Seven types of in-vehicle data are gathered in the main unit. Everything except camera data goes to the cloud in real time. For video, we receive only the segments where a risk was detected, and that video is deleted automatically after the retention period.

  2. 02

    Read

    Our own AI engine compares real-time data with the training data built up over the years and produces a risk index from 0 to 100. Each assessment takes 0.18 seconds.

  3. 03

    Alert

    When the risk index goes above 70, the system sends an automatic warning. Above 85, it responds right away at the highest risk level. Remote control responds in 0.81 seconds.
