Managed Operation

Hand us your vehicles and we run them

CAMVER takes on the work of running vehicles, from bookings and customer service to handover, return and maintenance. We operate vehicles for four Hyundai Motor Group companies (Hyundai Motor Company, Kia, Hyundai Engineering and Hyundai Glovis).

We currently operate 145 vehicles. To date, they have made 30,000 trips and served 90,000 users.

Smart Station

We look after vehicles at 9 stations across Korea

A Smart Station is where CAMVER vehicles are picked up, returned and inspected. We opened a station in Namyang, Hwaseong in 2023 and set up stations nationwide in 2024.

For partners who want to open a station, we work through the setup together, from the first consultation to the pilot run.

How we open a station

  1. Consultation

    We meet in person to talk it through.

  2. Site analysis

    We visit the site and review operating and management conditions in detail.

  3. Contract

    We sign on a standard quote or a custom proposal.

  4. Station setup

    We put in the facilities, equipment and software.

  5. Service training

    We train your team to run the station.

  6. Pilot service

    We do a trial run before the official launch.

  7. Grand opening

    The station officially starts service.
