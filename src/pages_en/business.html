Mobile Safety ShelterIn progress

A rest space that drives to sites with nowhere to take a break

It is a rest space built from a converted camper van. We send it to construction, logistics and facility management sites where a separate break room is hard to build. Its no-idle air conditioner runs with the engine off to keep workers cool.

We don't hand over the vehicle and walk away. CAMVER handles dispatch, maintenance, pickup and redeployment, and monitors every unit with Fleet AI OS.

What we monitor

  • Indoor temperature and humidity
  • CO and CO₂ levels
  • Vehicle location and site deployment status
  • Inspection and maintenance history

Special-purpose vehicles are changing

A special-purpose vehicle carries special equipment for a specific job. Camper vans, buses, trucks, ambulances, fire trucks, cranes, snowplows and mixer trucks all fall into this group.

As more vehicles run on electric power and electronic controls, real-time monitoring of vehicle condition matters more.

AreaUntil nowChangeGoing forward
DriveMechanical drive→Electric drive
PowerCombustion engine→Electric motor
EnergyFossil fuel→Electricity and hydrogen
ControlMechanical control→Electronic control
OperationStandalone operation→Intelligent, connected operation
