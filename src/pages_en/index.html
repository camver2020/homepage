We build, run and protect vehicles

CAMVER started in Seoul in 2019. We convert vehicles and run them ourselves, and we built Fleet AI OS, our AI safety monitoring system, on the driving data from that work. Today we operate 145 vehicles and have driven a total of 4.5 million km without an accident.

More about our solution

A vehicle's condition
in one number: Fleet AI OS

It combines the data each vehicle sends into a single risk index and alerts you before things get dangerous. When needed, it controls the vehicle remotely.

Accredited testing (KTCC) confirmed risk assessment in 0.18 seconds, 100% accuracy in detecting early signs of fire and remote control response in 0.81 seconds.
