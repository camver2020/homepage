Overview

Safety monitoring built by a company that runs its own vehicles

CAMVER opened in Seoul in December 2019. We started by renting out a few camper vans. Today we convert vehicles ourselves and run company vehicles on our clients' behalf.

The more vehicles we ran, the bigger the safety problem became. So we built our own monitoring system that reads vehicle data in real time and warns of risks early. That system is now Fleet AI OS, and it also runs on vehicles owned by other companies and public institutions.

CEO Message

Out in the field, we learned that vehicles can't afford to stop

CAMVER started in 2019 with a few camper vans. We fixed them and rented them out ourselves, and the first thing we learned was this: if you don't know what's happening in the vehicle your customer is riding in, you can't promise them safety.

So we built a monitoring system for our own use. The data from six years of running 145 vehicles became what is now Fleet AI OS. We have built vehicles, run them and kept them safe, all in one company, so we know what people in the field really need.

At CAMVER, we want to build a happier future through technology and culture. We will keep putting our energy into making travel safer.

CEO, CAMVER Inc.Youngsin Kim
