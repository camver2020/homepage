1. Purposes of processing personal information

The Company processes personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, the Company will obtain consent in advance.

  • Service and partnership inquiries: reviewing inquiries, replying and contacting you for consultation, providing quotes and proposals
  • Job applications: verifying applicants, reviewing documents, informing applicants about hiring steps such as interviews
  • Partnership applications: reviewing proposals, contacting the person in charge

2. Personal information items processed

CategoryRequiredOptional
Service and partnership inquiriesInquiry type, contact person's name, phone number, inquiry detailsCompany or organization name, email

Please do not include sensitive information, such as resident registration numbers or health information, in your inquiry or attachments. The Company does not request such information and deletes it as soon as it is found.

3. Processing and retention periods

CategoryRetention period
Service and partnership inquiries1 year from the date of receipt. If the inquiry leads to a contract, until the contractual relationship ends
Job applications1 year from the date of receipt. Deleted immediately if the applicant requests deletion. If the applicant is hired, for the period of HR management
Partnership applications1 year from the date of receipt. If a partnership agreement is signed, until the contractual relationship ends

Where other laws set a retention period, the information is stored separately for that period.

4. Provision of personal information to third parties

The Company does not provide personal information to third parties, except where the data subject has consented or where a law makes special provision for it.

7. Procedure and method for destroying personal information

When the retention period ends or the purpose of processing is achieved, the Company destroys the personal information without delay. Submission records, attachments and notification emails are permanently deleted so that they cannot be recovered, and printed documents are shredded.

8. Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them

Data subjects may at any time request access, correction, deletion and suspension of processing of their personal information, and may withdraw their consent.

  • If you send a request by email or phone to the Chief Privacy Officer listed in section 11, the Company will handle it without delay and inform you of the result.
  • You may also make a request through a legal representative or a person you have authorized. In that case, a power of attorney must be submitted.
  • Deletion cannot be requested for information that other laws require to be retained, and a request to suspend processing may be refused as permitted by law. If a request is refused, the Company will inform you of the reason.

The Company does not accept job applications or inquiries from children under the age of 14.

9. Measures to ensure security

  • Administrative measures: Access to applications is limited to the staff in charge.
  • Technical measures: The website and the transmission of applications are encrypted with HTTPS, and two-step verification is applied to the account where applications are stored.
  • Record keeping: A change history is kept for the documents where applications are stored.

10. Installation, operation and refusal of automatic collection tools

The CAMVER website does not use cookies, analytics tools or targeted advertising tools.

However, because fonts (Google Fonts) and how-to video thumbnails (YouTube) are loaded from each company's servers, your IP address and browser information may be sent to Google when you open a page. Clicking a video playlist link takes you to the YouTube website, where Google's privacy policy applies from that point on.
